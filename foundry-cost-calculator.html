Notes & assumptions

Rates are published Azure AI Foundry pay-as-you-go prices, est. June 2026, US/global-standard deployment — they vary by region/deployment type (global/data-zone/PTU) and change often, so every rate is editable. PTU & commitment tiers (not modelled) can cut effective costs materially.

Foundry figures are measured (gpt-4.1, 8 DripOCP docs → 100k pages): 227.57M LLM tokens (94.4% input), 2.6M embedding tokens, 100,000 OCR pages. A 20% safety buffer is applied to the token estimates (LLM + embed, not OCR pages) to match the slideshow methodology — set it to 0 to see raw measured cost.
TTA figures are from TTA-Cost-Analysis.png: combined Extraction + Classification = 295.0M tokens (includes their 20% buffer); input share 65.8% derived from their stated 2,500-in / 1,300-out per-page rate. TTA runs MSDI (Document Intelligence) on Computershare's Foundry, so the OCR line is like-for-like with our solution — same 100,000 pages, same rate, same free tier (it cancels out of the comparison). No KB-grounding embeddings are modelled on the TTA side.
